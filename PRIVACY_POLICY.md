# Privacy Policy for StarX Defender

**Last Updated:** May 15, 2026

## Introduction

StarX Defender is a mobile arcade game. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our app.

## Data Collection

### Information We Collect

#### 1. **Sensor Data (Accelerometer)**
- We access your device's accelerometer sensor to enable game controls
- Sensor data is processed locally on your device in real-time for gameplay mechanics
- Accelerometer data is **not stored** or transmitted to our servers

#### 2. **Firebase Services**
Our app uses Firebase (Google's platform) for:
- **Authentication:** Anonymous user authentication to track game progress
- **Database:** Cloud Firestore to store your game data (high scores, progress, game stats) associated with your anonymous user ID
- **Analytics:** Firebase Analytics to understand app usage patterns and improve gameplay experience

**Data transmitted to Firebase includes:**
- Game performance metrics (scores, levels completed, playtime)
- Device information (OS version, device model for analytics)
- Custom game events (level completion, purchases)

#### 3. **Google Mobile Ads**
- The app displays personalized and non-personalized advertisements
- Google AdMob may collect device identifiers and ad interaction data
- We follow Google's policies on ad delivery and data handling

#### 4. **Audio and Storage**
- **Audio Permissions:** Used to play game music and sound effects
- **Local Storage:** Game preferences, settings, and progress are stored locally on your device
- **External Storage:** Used for caching game assets and media files

#### 5. **Internet Connection**
- Required to communicate with Firebase services and display ads
- All communication uses HTTPS encryption

### What We Do NOT Collect
- **No Personal Information:** We do not request or collect personal information like name, email, or phone number
- **No Location Data:** The app does not access your GPS location
- **No Contacts:** The app does not access your contact list
- **No Camera/Microphone:** The app does not access camera or microphone beyond audio playback

## Data Usage

- **Game Data:** Your scores, progress, and statistics are stored to maintain your gameplay history
- **Analytics:** We analyze aggregated, anonymized data to improve the game experience
- **Advertising:** Ad networks use data to serve relevant advertisements
- **Service Improvement:** Firebase data helps us identify bugs, optimize performance, and enhance features

## Data Sharing

- **Firebase:** Your game data is stored with Google Firebase
- **Google AdMob:** Ad engagement data is shared with Google's advertising network
- **Third-party Services:** We do not intentionally share personal data with other third parties
- **Legal Requirements:** We may disclose data if required by law

## Data Security

- All data is encrypted during transmission using HTTPS
- Game data stored locally uses device-level security mechanisms
- Firebase provides enterprise-grade security infrastructure
- We implement industry-standard security practices

## Data Retention

- **Local Data:** Remains on your device until you uninstall the app
- **Firebase Data:** Associated with your anonymous account; you may contact us to request data deletion
- **Analytics Data:** Retained per Google Firebase default retention policies (typically 14 months)

## Your Privacy Rights

- You can delete local game data by clearing the app's cache/data through device settings
- You can opt-out of personalized ads through your device's advertising settings
- For Firebase data deletion requests, please contact us

## Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

- StarX Defender is suitable for all ages, including children under 13
- We do not knowingly collect personal information from children
- Parents/guardians can request deletion of their child's game data
- Personalized advertising can be disabled in device settings
